# Pitch Deck Outline

Copy this into `founder/fundraising/deck.md` and fill in the blanks. One idea per
slide, one line per idea. The order is the default and it matters — each slide
earns the next. Cut any slide you cannot fill honestly rather than padding it.

Before you send anything: every number below should have a source written next to
it in your working copy (a Stripe export, a cohort, a signed pilot). A figure you
cannot trace does not go on a slide.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

> **[Company]** — [the one-line description]
> [Founder name] · [contact] · [date]

*The one-liner should let a stranger repeat what you do. If it needs a second
sentence, it is not ready.*

---

## Slide 2 — Problem

> **[The person] [has this pain] [this often].**
> Today they [the workaround they use], which costs them [time / money / risk].

Fill in a specific person and a specific moment, not a market statistic. *(Who
hurts, how often, and what it costs them now.)*

---

## Slide 3 — Insight

> **Everyone assumes [common belief]. In fact, [the non-obvious truth].**
> That is why [the existing alternatives] fail and [your approach] works.

*This is the slide that makes the rest credible. If you cannot state the insight,
you have a feature, not a thesis.*

---

## Slide 4 — Product

> **[What it does, in one breath].**
> [Screenshot or 20-second demo link.]

Tie it back to the insight on Slide 3. Show, do not narrate.

---

## Slide 5 — Traction

> [The most advanced credible number you have, with the trend.]
> - [Metric 1]: [value] — [change vs last period]
> - [Metric 2]: [value] — [change vs last period]
> - [Proof point]: [customer names / segments / a quote]

*See `references/pitch-and-updates.md` §3 for what to show at pre-seed, seed, and
post-seed. Prefer a trend or cohort over a headline.*

---

## Slide 6 — Market

> **Who:** [the specific buyer].
> **How many / how reachable:** [bottom-up: buyers × price, or channels and their size].
> **Why now:** [the change that makes this the right moment].

Size it from the bottom up, not as a percentage of a large industry number.

---

## Slide 7 — Business model

> **Price:** [what a customer pays, on what unit].
> **Cost to serve:** [the main cost per customer].
> **Why the economics work:** [the path to a healthy margin].

---

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market

> **Channels that have worked:** [name what actually produced customers].
> **The repeatable motion we scale with this money:** [the specific plan].

Name what you have actually done, not a channel wishlist.

---

## Slide 9 — Team

> **[Founder]** — [the relevant advantage: lived the problem / has the audience /
> shipped this before].
> **What we are missing and how we cover it:** [the honest gap and the plan].

For a solo founder, the advantage and the honesty are the whole slide.

---

## Slide 10 — The ask

> **Raising:** [amount] at [pre-money] pre-money ([post-money] post).
> **Instrument:** [priced equity | SAFE | convertible note].
> **This round buys:** [N months] of runway to reach **[provable milestone]**,
> which unlocks **[next round / default alive]**.

*This is the Step 2 sentence. Run `scripts/raise_math.py` before you quote the
number so you know the dilution.*

---

## Slide 11 — Use of funds

> - [Hiring]: [x]%
> - [Product]: [x]%
> - [Go-to-market]: [x]%
> - [Infrastructure / other]: [x]%

Each line should tie to reaching the milestone on Slide 10.

---

## Appendix (optional)

Keep supporting detail here, not in the main deck: cohort charts, the detailed
cap table, a longer financial model, references. Appendices are for the questions
that come up in the meeting, not for the story itself.

---

## Pre-send check

- [ ] Every number traces to a real source (named next to it).
- [ ] The ask ties to a milestone, and the milestone is provable.
- [ ] The dilution matches `scripts/raise_math.py`.
- [ ] A stranger can repeat the business after reading Slides 2–4.
- [ ] Nothing here is a claim I could not defend in diligence.
- [ ] **The founder owns every number and term in this deck; the skill only drafts it.**
