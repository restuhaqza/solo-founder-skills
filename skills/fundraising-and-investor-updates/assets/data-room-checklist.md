# Data Room Checklist

Work this into `founder/fundraising/data-room/`. Complete the **Essential** items
before diligence starts; mark the rest **As asked** so you are organized rather
than scrambling. Two rules throughout: **index it** (one place, clear filenames,
a short `README.md`) and **read everything you put in** — you are accountable for
every document.

---

## 0. Index

- [ ] `README.md` at the top listing every folder and what it holds.
- [ ] A "last updated" date on each item so staleness is visible.
- [ ] Nothing in here you have not read end to end.

---

## 1. Company (Essential)

- [ ] Incorporation documents and current good standing / registration.
- [ ] Founding/operating agreement or bylaws.
- [ ] **Cap table — current, and fully diluted** (options and any SAFEs/notes).
- [ ] Running **SAFE / convertible-note stack**: amount, cap, discount, and any
      MFN clause for each. *(A confusing stack is why leads walk — keep it in one file.)*
- [ ] Board / consent / shareholder records.
- [ ] Any outstanding convertible instruments not yet on the cap table.

## 2. Financials (Essential)

- [ ] Revenue by month (a clean export).
- [ ] P&L or a simple summary — revenue, costs, and the resulting net.
- [ ] Bank statements for the business account.
- [ ] Payment-processor export (Stripe / merchant-of-record).
- [ ] Any tax filings or registrations, and current set-aside status.

## 3. Product & metrics (Essential)

- [ ] A short "what the product does" note, tied to the pitch insight.
- [ ] **Metric definitions** — exactly how MRR, activation, and churn are counted.
- [ ] Dashboards or exports behind the numbers shown in the deck.
- [ ] Cohorts behind the retention claims.

## 4. Customers & contracts (Essential, as available)

- [ ] Customer list, or clear segments if names are confidential.
- [ ] **Revenue-concentration picture** — how much comes from the top few.
- [ ] Key customer agreements or letters of intent.
- [ ] The churn breakdown: voluntary vs involuntary.

## 5. Team & IP (Essential)

- [ ] Founder agreement and any equity-grant documents.
- [ ] Contractor and **IP assignment** agreements for anyone who wrote code or content.
- [ ] Material third-party and open-source dependencies in use.

## 6. Legal & risk (Mixed)

- [ ] Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (as published).
- [ ] Any insurance policies.
- [ ] **As asked:** security questionnaire responses, SOC 2 / compliance docs,
      detailed IP schedules, anything a lawyer has flagged.
- [ ] A short list of open legal questions for a professional to answer.

---

## Before you hand it over — quick check

- [ ] Every **Essential** item is present or has a dated note explaining the gap.
- [ ] The cap table matches `scripts/raise_math.py` (fully diluted, pool included).
- [ ] Nothing was added that you have not read.
- [ ] Hard-to-fill items are marked "as asked", not silently missing.
- [ ] **Terms, filings, and legal wording are for a professional — the founder decides.**
