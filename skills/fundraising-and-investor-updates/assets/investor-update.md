# Investor Update Template

Copy to `founder/fundraising/updates/<YYYY-MM>.md` and send on the same day every
month — good news or bad. Same shape every time. Keep it short enough that an
investor reads it in two minutes.

Numbers come from `product-metrics`; pull them, do not re-derive them here. The
founder writes the note in their own voice and sends it — this is a draft.

---

**Subject:** `<Company> — <Month> update` (add a short honest headline if the
month was hard: "a tough month, here's the plan")

---

- [Outcome, not activity — with a number where you have one.]

## Lowlights / what's hard

- [The miss, stated plainly and early. If there is no honest lowlight, look

## What I'm doing about it

- [The plan for the lowlight, with a date where you can.]

## Asks — one clear ask

- **[One specific, answerable request: an intro, a candidate, a customer, a piece
  of advice.]**

*(One ask gets answered. Five get skimmed.)*

## Thanks

[Short, genuine close — a specific thank-you beats a generic one.]

— [Name]

---

## Before you send — quick check

- [ ] The numbers match last month's definitions and `product-metrics`.
- [ ] The lowlight is present, plain, and early — no hiding bad news.
- [ ] There is exactly one ask, and it is specific enough to answer.
- [ ] An investor can read this without you re-explaining the business.
- [ ] It is going out on the scheduled day, not only when there is good news.
- [ ] **The founder sends this — no one else.**
