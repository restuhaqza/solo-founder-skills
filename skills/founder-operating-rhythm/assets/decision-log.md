# Decision Log

One row per meaningful decision. The point of the **Type** column is to spend
your deliberation where it counts: **Type 1** decisions are irreversible or very
expensive to undo (entity choice, a pricing change affecting existing customers,
killing a channel, a big build) and deserve slow, written thought. **Type 2**
decisions are reversible and cheap to undo (most features, experiments, copy,
tool choices) and should be made fast — the cost of deliberating usually exceeds
the cost of being wrong. Founders tend to grind Type 2 calls and rush Type 1.

## Revisit queue

_Anything past its "Revisit on" date and still not decided. Pull these into the
weekly review. Two cycles on the same stuck Type 1 decision is a signal to
decide with the information you have, or to name what you are waiting for._
