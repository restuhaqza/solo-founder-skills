## Day-of checklist

- [ ] Listing scheduled; timezone checked (PH days run on Pacific time)
- [ ] First comment drafted and ready to paste
- [ ] A "thank you + answer anything" reply pattern ready for the comment stream
- [ ] Bug triage list open; someone (you) watching for blockers
- [ ] The whole day blocked — do not launch and disappear
- [ ] 3–5 people who will genuinely try it (not just upvote) lined up

## Etiquette

- Never ask for upvotes directly; ask people to **use it and give feedback**.
- Reply to every comment, including criticism — with substance, not defensiveness.
- Don't bury bugs; acknowledge and fix them in the thread.
- Post once; do not spam the listing link across other communities with identical copy.

## After the day

- [ ] Send a personal note to anyone who gave detailed feedback
- [ ] Onboard the signups who never activated
- [ ] Publish a specific build-in-public post: what PH actually produced (real numbers)
- [ ] Log results by source via your UTMs so the channel is attributable

## Boundary

The founder writes and posts the listing and replies. The agent drafts; it does not submit, comment, or send on the founder's behalf.
