# Support Macros — fill-in template

Copy this to `founder/support/macros.md` and edit. Keep the set small and true:
five to eight macros covering the questions you actually receive. Delete a macro
the moment its underlying issue is fixed or a doc now handles it.

Rules: personalise the first line (greet by name, reference the actual ask). Never
send a raw macro to a frustrated customer, and never use a macro past the
acknowledgement for anything billing- or cancellation-related. Write macros from
real tickets, not imagination.

Placeholders: `[USER_NAME]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[FOUNDER_NAME]`, `[LINK]`, `[PLAN]`,
`[DATE]`.

---

## 1. How-to / "how do I …?"

**Use when:** a customer asks how to do a routine action the doc now covers.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Here's how to [ACTION] in [PRODUCT]:
>
> 1. [STEP ONE]
> 2. [STEP TWO]
> 3. [STEP THREE]
>
> Full walkthrough with screenshots: [LINK]
>
> If that doesn't match what you see, tell me what's on your screen and I'll
> take a look.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

*Deflect upgrade:* if this is asked often, the doc link should be findable by
search — if it isn't, the doc title is the problem.

---

## 2. Bug report / "it's broken"

**Use when:** a customer reports something that clearly isn't working as
intended. Acknowledge fast; do not promise a fix time you can't keep.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Thanks for flagging this — that's not how it should behave, and I'm sorry it
> cost you time. I've reproduced it on my side. I'll follow up here as soon as
> there's a fix.
>
> Anything else it's blocking in the meantime? I can suggest a workaround.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

*Limit:* only use the "reproduced" line if you actually reproduced it. Otherwise
write "I'm looking into it now."

---

## 3. Feature request / "could you add …?"

**Use when:** a customer asks for something the product doesn't do.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Thanks for this — [FEATURE] is a fair ask, and I've logged it with the reason
> you gave, which is what helps me prioritise. I can't promise a date, but I'll
> tell you if it ships.
>
> In the meantime, the closest thing today is [WORKAROUND / RELATED FEATURE].
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

*Log it* in `founder/support/feedback.md` and score it Impact × Votes ÷ Effort.
Don't chase the loudest single voice.

---

## 4. Billing — acknowledgement (human follow-up required)

**Use when:** a billing question or dispute arrives. Send the acknowledgement
same-day, then handle the substance by hand. Never let a macro close a money
question.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Got your billing question — I'm looking at your account now and will reply
> properly today. Nothing further you need to do.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

*Limit:* this is an acknowledgement only. The real reply is typed, specific, and
hits your ≤24h target. Refunds and plan changes are your call, never the macro's.

---

## 5. Cancellation — acknowledgement and one honest question

**Use when:** a cancellation arrives. Respond same-day; treat it as product
research, not a retention fight.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Understood — I've cancelled [PRODUCT] for you and you won't be charged again.
> If you have ten seconds, one question: what would have made it worth staying?
> No pitch, it just genuinely shapes what I build next.
>
> Thanks for giving it a try.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

*Limit:* never substitute a discount for a real answer. A fix earns a return; a
discount trains waiting.

---

## 6. Refund / failed payment

**Use when:** a failed card or a refund request. Payment-saver is plumbing (the
dunning sequence); a refund is a judgement call you make by hand.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> Your payment didn't go through, which usually just means a card needs
> updating. One-click fix here: [LINK]. I've kept your account active while you
> sort it out.
>
> If you'd rather not continue, just reply and I'll handle it.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

---

## 7. "Not sure how to start" / onboarding nudge

**Use when:** a new signup replies to the onboarding sequence, stuck. This is the
onboarding-rescue playbook, hand-written.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> The fastest first step is [SINGLE ACTION]. Here's the direct link: [LINK].
>
> If you tell me what you're trying to get done, I'll point you at the shortest
> path to [ACTIVATION EVENT].
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

---

## 8. Thanks / positive note

**Use when:** a customer says something nice. Short and real; ask for nothing.

> Hi [USER_NAME],
>
> That made my day — thank you. If [PRODUCT] keeps being useful, the best thing
> you can do is tell one person who has the same problem.
>
> — [FOUNDER_NAME]

---

## Review

Re-read this file once a week against your actual tickets. Every question you
answered by hand twice belongs here or in a doc. Every macro whose issue got
fixed gets deleted.
